The Ember Hearth

How to play

Dawn Whitmore keeps a tavern and remembers what you do. Play her five-scene arc with the suggested lines or your own words, then keep talking once it is done. Everything runs on a stub model in your browser, so nothing here needs a network or a graphics card.

The point is the panel on the right. It is the real research instrument, reading the actual memory system as it answers each turn.
