How much of this is machine checked

The build reads numbers straight out of the run, and of them are cross-checked a second time against the prose of docs/findings.md. If either disagrees, the page is not written. That is the only reason to trust a generated results page over a hand written one.

Numbers marked * come from analyses that write no run artefact, so they are quoted from docs/findings.md rather than verified here. They are reproducible from the menu: the content grid, the signal attribution, and the rater agreement each have their own command.

This page is generated by assets/build_results.py and opens with no server and no network. The full record, with every caveat, lives in docs/findings.md. The scorer is touchable in the demo.
