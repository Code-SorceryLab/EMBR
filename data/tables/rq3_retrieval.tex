% Comparisons are exact paired sign-flip permutation tests against embr_tuned, Holm-Bonferroni
% corrected within family (primary, ablation, secondary) rather than pooled. Read every p against
% its own attainable_p_floor: a comparison whose floor is at or above 0.05 could not have reached
% significance under any arrangement of its own data, so a corrected p of 1.0 there records absent
